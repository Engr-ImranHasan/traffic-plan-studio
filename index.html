QUICK GUIDE

Plan routes, clearly.

Build a traffic management plan with editable routes, restricted areas, gates and safety notes.

  1. Find your siteUse the search box or enter coordinates to move the map.
  2. Add plan itemsChoose Route, Area, Marker, Text or Hazard. Click the map to place points; double-click to finish a route or area.
  3. Edit any itemSelect its name in Plan Items to change its label, color, line style or notes.
  4. Export for freeSave your plan as JSON, export an editable SVG/PNG or choose Print / Save as PDF.
Traffic planning diagrams are for personal planning. Verify routes, access and safety requirements on site.
